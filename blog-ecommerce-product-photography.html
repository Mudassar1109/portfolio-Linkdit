Frequently asked questions

Where should I start?

Start by documenting the current setup and identifying the problem that has the greatest effect on visitors or business operations. Make one change at a time and verify it.

Will following this guide guarantee Google rankings?

No. SEO depends on many factors and no checklist guarantees indexing, rankings or traffic. Focus on useful content, technical accessibility and ongoing measurement.

How often should I review this?

Review after significant site changes and set a recurring maintenance schedule that fits the website's complexity and business requirements.
