Shopify Cart Abandonment: 7 Checkout Frictions That Cost Orders

A practical store-owner guide to unexpected costs, delivery uncertainty, account walls and confusing checkout flows.

By Muhammad Mudassar Elahi · Updated November 20, 2026

A customer can like a product, add it to the cart and still leave before paying. The problem is not always price or traffic quality. Checkout friction—especially surprise costs, unclear delivery information and forced account creation—can interrupt a purchase at the final step.

1. The benchmark: carts are often abandoned

Baymard Institute reports a global average cart-abandonment rate of 70.19%, based on its compilation of studies. This is an industry benchmark, not a prediction for every store. Your own rate depends on product category, device mix, traffic intent, shipping policy and how the metric is calculated.

2. Seven issues worth checking

1) Shipping or taxes appear late. 2) Delivery estimates are missing. 3) Returns are unclear. 4) Checkout requires account creation. 5) Payment methods do not match customer expectations. 6) Form fields are excessive or confusing. 7) Trust and support details disappear at checkout.

3. Shopify fixes to test

Show shipping thresholds and delivery expectations before checkout, keep product and cart totals understandable, configure the payment methods available to your business, use clear policy pages, test guest checkout settings where available, and remove unnecessary apps or popups from the purchase journey.

4. Measure before changing

Track product views, add-to-cart events, checkout starts and purchases. Segment by device and acquisition source. If many visitors add to cart but few begin checkout, inspect cart clarity; if checkout starts are healthy but purchases drop, investigate payment, delivery and form friction.

5. Find the exact drop-off stage

Use analytics events to distinguish add-to-cart, checkout-start and purchase. A weak add-to-cart rate can indicate product-page issues; a weak checkout-start rate can point to cart friction; a weak purchase rate after checkout begins can indicate payment, shipping or form problems. Validate event tracking before trusting the funnel.

6. Prioritize fixes by margin and customer impact

Unexpected shipping charges, unclear delivery timing and limited payment choices can undermine trust. Estimate the potential benefit of a fix, but do not assume every abandoned cart is recoverable—some visitors are researching, comparing or simply not ready to buy.

Frequently asked questions

Can a Shopify app automatically fix abandonment?

No app can fix every cause. Diagnose where customers leave, then test a specific change.

Should I offer discounts to every abandoned cart?

Not automatically. First remove avoidable friction; blanket discounts can reduce margin and train customers to wait.
