Privacy Policy

How information is handled across this website, project enquiries and M~M Rewards.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Who operates this website

M~M Web Development is operated by Muhammad Mudassar Elahi. For privacy questions or requests, contact elahimudassasr1@gmail.com.

2. Information you choose to provide

If you submit the project enquiry form, the website prepares your message and opens WhatsApp so you can send it. The form itself does not submit the message to a website-owned contact database. WhatsApp receives information only if you choose to send the message through its service.

If you use M~M Rewards, you sign in through Google authentication provided by Supabase. The application uses your authenticated account identifier and basic profile details, such as your name, email address and profile image when available, to identify your account and associate reward activity with it.

3. Rewards information

When you use Spin & Win, the rewards service records spin attempts, dates, outcomes and any reward code issued. If you claim a reward for a package, the selected package and calculated discount details may also be stored. The admin interface can show reward codes and their status to an authorized rewards administrator. Do not submit sensitive personal information in reward fields.

4. How information is used

  • To respond to enquiries you choose to send.
  • To authenticate your M~M Rewards account and maintain your reward history.
  • To apply daily spin limits, issue and validate reward codes, and administer reward status.
  • To protect the service, troubleshoot errors and maintain website functionality.

5. Service providers and external links

The website relies on third-party services and resources, including Google sign-in, Supabase authentication/database/edge functions, WhatsApp, and externally hosted fonts, icons or images. Those providers may process technical or account information under their own privacy terms. When you follow an external link, the destination website's privacy policy applies.

6. Cookies and browser storage

Authentication providers may use browser storage or cookies to maintain a secure sign-in session. This website does not describe or promise a complete cookie inventory here; browser behaviour can depend on the third-party services you use. You can manage cookies and site storage through your browser settings, although clearing them may sign you out or affect site functionality.

7. Retention and security

Information is retained for as long as it is needed to operate the relevant feature, meet legitimate operational needs or resolve disputes. Exact retention periods depend on the underlying service configuration. Reasonable technical measures are used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and requests

You may choose not to use the contact form, Google sign-in or rewards features. To ask about access, correction or deletion of information associated with your account, email us from the address connected to the account and describe your request. Some records may need to be retained for security, fraud prevention or legitimate operational reasons. We will assess requests against applicable requirements and the capabilities of the underlying providers.

9. Children's privacy

This website and its services are intended for general business and professional audiences, not specifically for children. Do not use the rewards feature or submit personal information if you are not permitted to do so under the applicable service terms.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website or its services change. The latest version and revision date will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact elahimudassasr1@gmail.com.
